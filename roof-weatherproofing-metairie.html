Weatherproofing on a Metairie roof means building it to hold together in the wind loads of a Gulf hurricane and shed wind driven rain even when a few shingles come off. Standard code minimum roof construction meets the ordinary case. Weatherproofed construction adds redundancy: adhesive underlayment that seals the deck when shingles fail, ring shank nails that hold in higher wind pressure, and sealed edges that resist water uplift.

The Insurance Institute for Business and Home Safety (IBHS) FORTIFIED standard is the recognised weatherproofing benchmark, and Louisiana's Fortify Homes Program pays a grant of up to $10,000 to upgrade a Metairie roof to that standard. On top of the grant, most Louisiana carriers offer premium discounts of 10 to 30 percent on the wind portion of the policy for a FORTIFIED designated roof.

Why sealing the roof deck matters most

The single most valuable weatherproofing upgrade is a fully sealed roof deck. Adhesive underlayment or taped seams keep the roof watertight even when 40 to 60 percent of shingles come off in a Category 3 hurricane. Studies from IBHS after Katrina and Ida show sealed deck homes had dramatically lower interior water damage than code minimum homes.

Sealed deck is the first FORTIFIED requirement and where most of the actual storm resilience comes from.

Weatherproofing is cheapest during a re roof

Adhesive underlayment, upgraded fasteners, and sealed edges are cheapest to install during a full re roof because the deck is exposed anyway. Adding FORTIFIED to a re roof typically adds $2,000 to $4,000 over standard installation. Doing it later as a standalone job requires stripping the existing roof to reach the deck, which costs more.

Big Easy Roofing prices the FORTIFIED upgrade alongside any Metairie re roof scope, so the grant and LDI discount can be weighed against the standard scope.

The FORTIFIED Roof upgrades that matter most

Three upgrades deliver most of the storm resilience in a FORTIFIED Roof designation. Sealed roof deck keeps water out when shingles fail. Ring shank nails on a tight pattern hold shingles in higher wind. Enhanced edge details resist wind uplift at the roof perimeter. Each is required for the IBHS designation and each is verified by the third party evaluator.

Sealed roof deck

The single most valuable weatherproofing upgrade. Adhesive underlayment or taped seams keep water out even when hurricane winds strip 40 to 60 percent of shingles. FORTIFIED requires either fully self adhering underlayment across the entire deck, or 4 inch wide tape along every deck sheathing joint plus standard underlayment over it.

Big Easy Roofing installs whichever meets the spec at the lowest cost for the specific Metairie roof.

Ring shank nail pattern

FORTIFIED requires ring shank nails (not smooth shank) on a 6 nail pattern for asphalt shingles, and a tighter 4 inch on centre nailing spacing along ridge and eave edges. Ring shank nails hold three to four times the withdrawal load of smooth shank nails in high wind.

The upgrade is largely a materials change, not a labour change, so the cost delta over standard installation is modest and mostly worth doing on any re roof regardless of FORTIFIED certification.

Enhanced starter and ridge cap

Wind uplift on a Metairie roof concentrates at the eave and the ridge. FORTIFIED requires factory laminated starter strips at the eave (not field cut from 3 tab shingles), and enhanced ridge cap that meets the same wind rating as the field shingles.

Together with the sealed deck and ring shank nails, these three upgrades are what earn the IBHS designation certificate.

How long does a Metairie weatherproofing job take

Basic weatherproofing during a re roof adds no extra time to the re roof itself. Standalone weatherproofing over an existing roof takes one to three working days. FORTIFIED certification adds a separate 2 to 4 hour evaluator visit after installation, and the certificate takes a week or two to issue.

How much does roof weatherproofing cost in Metairie

Estimated ranges. Basic weatherproofing runs $1,500 to $3,000. FORTIFIED Roof certified runs $3,000 to $6,000, offset by up to $10,000 in Louisiana Fortify Homes Program grant. FORTIFIED Silver with opening protection runs $6,000 to $15,000 or more. Ice and water shield only runs $800 to $2,000.

What is the Louisiana Fortify Homes Program grant

A Louisiana state grant of up to $10,000 that pays for upgrading a qualifying home's roof to the IBHS FORTIFIED Roof standard. Awarded through the Louisiana Department of Insurance, must be awarded before work starts, and typically covers most or all of the FORTIFIED upgrade cost above a standard re roof.

Do I get an insurance discount for a FORTIFIED roof

Yes. The Louisiana Department of Insurance recognises FORTIFIED designations for premium discounts of typically 10 to 30 percent on the wind portion of the homeowners policy. Exact figure is set by the carrier. Discount applies for the life of the FORTIFIED designation, so the savings compound year after year.

Who qualifies for the Fortify Homes Program grant

Louisiana homeowners with a primary residence in an eligible parish (Jefferson Parish included), insured on the property, meeting income and program criteria. Grant must be applied for before work starts, and the roof must be certified to FORTIFIED Roof standard by an IBHS approved evaluator. Big Easy Roofing walks through eligibility on the free consultation.

What does an IBHS evaluator do

An IBHS approved evaluator is an independent third party inspector who verifies the roof meets the FORTIFIED standard: sealed deck, ring shank nailing pattern, enhanced starter and ridge cap, and correctly installed underlayment. The evaluator visit takes 2 to 4 hours and costs $500 to $900. Big Easy Roofing coordinates the visit.

Can I add weatherproofing to my existing Metairie roof

Basic weatherproofing (adhesive underlayment, upgraded fasteners) has to go under the shingles, so it usually happens during a re roof. Some elements like ice and water shield along eaves can be added by stripping and replacing local sections. FORTIFIED certification requires a full re roof to the FORTIFIED spec.

Is FORTIFIED worth it if I never have a hurricane

The Louisiana Department of Insurance premium discount alone (10 to 30 percent on the wind portion for the life of the designation) typically pays back the FORTIFIED upgrade cost within 5 to 10 years, before any storm ever hits. On a Gulf Coast home, the risk math also favours FORTIFIED given the actual hurricane frequency.
