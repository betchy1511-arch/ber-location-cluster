Jefferson Parish permit and code checklist

Metairie is unincorporated, so the parish permits your roof rather than the City of New Orleans. Four requirements, all handled by us.

1

$50 permit, no plans

Residential re roofing permit, flat fee, paid by credit card through MGO Connect. No construction plans and no survey required.

2

Licence at $7,500 or more

From 1 January 2026 residential roof work of $7,500 or more requires a Louisiana Residential Roofing or Residential Construction licence.

3

2021 IRC compliance

Re roofing must meet the 2021 International Residential Code for nailing, underlayment and edge detail.

4

Geo tagged phase photos

No inspector attends. Every phase is documented with the nailing pattern visible and material packaging showing its wind rating.

Source: Jefferson Parish residential re roofing permit requirements, jeffparish.gov, and the Louisiana Fortify Homes Program, ldi.la.gov. Permit fee, licence threshold, code edition and grant terms as of August 2026 and subject to change

Metairie roof replacement questions

How much does a new roof cost in Metairie

Most full architectural asphalt replacements land between $7,000 and $18,000 depending on size, pitch and how many layers come off. Larger or complex roofs and standing seam metal run $16,000 to $28,000. The parish permit adds a flat $50.

Do I need a permit to replace a roof in Metairie

Yes. Metairie sits in unincorporated Jefferson Parish, which requires a residential re roofing permit. It is $50, paid by card through MGO Connect, with no construction plans and no survey required.

Will an inspector come to my house

No. The parish verifies re roofing from geo tagged photographs of every phase instead of a site visit, with the nailing pattern visible and the material packaging showing its wind rating.

Can I get the $10,000 state grant

The Louisiana Fortify Homes Program grants up to $10,000 toward the construction cost of a roof built to the FORTIFIED standard. You must be awarded before work starts, and it runs as a lottery with limited application windows.

Is metal worth it in Metairie

On a long hold it usually is. Standing seam runs roughly double the asphalt price and lasts roughly twice as long, and it handles low slope sections that shingles do not. On a five year hold, asphalt is the better number.
