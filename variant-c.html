Jefferson Parish permit and code checklist

Metairie is unincorporated, so Jefferson Parish permits your roof rather than the City of New Orleans. Four requirements, all handled by us.

Permit, no plans

$50 flat

Replacing 50 percent or more of the roof covering on a one or two family home in unincorporated Jefferson Parish. Paid by credit card through MGO Connect, with no construction plans and no survey required.

State licence rule

$7,500 or more

From 1 January 2026 residential roof work of $7,500 or more requires a Louisiana Residential Roofing or Residential Construction licence.

Building code

2021 IRC

Jefferson Parish requires re roofing to meet the 2021 International Residential Code for nailing pattern, underlayment and edge detail.

Photo verification

No routine site visit

Jefferson Parish verifies from geo tagged photographs. Every phase is documented with the nailing pattern visible and the material packaging showing its wind rating.

Source: Jefferson Parish residential re roofing permit requirements, jeffparish.gov, the Louisiana State Uniform Construction Code, lsuccc.dps.louisiana.gov, La. R.S. 37:2156.4, and the Louisiana Fortify Homes Program, ldi.la.gov. Permit fee, licence threshold, code edition and grant terms as of September 2026 and subject to change. The $50 fee, the photo verification process and the 2021 IRC requirement above are confirmed for unincorporated Jefferson Parish. Permit requirements and government fees vary by address and project, so we confirm them with the permitting authority and itemise them in your written proposal.

How much does a new roof cost in Metairie

Most full architectural asphalt replacements land between $7,000 and $18,000 depending on size, pitch and how many layers come off. Larger or complex roofs and standing seam metal run $16,000 to $28,000. The parish permit adds a flat $50.

Do I need a permit to replace a roof in Metairie

Yes. Metairie sits in unincorporated Jefferson Parish, which requires a residential re roofing permit. It is $50, paid by card through MGO Connect, with no construction plans and no survey required.

Will an inspector come to my house

No. The parish verifies re roofing from geo tagged photographs of every phase instead of a site visit, with the nailing pattern visible and the material packaging showing its wind rating.

Can I get the $10,000 state grant

The Louisiana Fortify Homes Program grants up to $10,000 toward the construction cost of a roof built to the FORTIFIED standard. You need programme approval before you choose a contractor or start work, and it runs in funded rounds with limited application windows, so it is not always open. Check ldi.la.gov for the current round before you plan around it.

Is metal worth it in Metairie

On a long hold it usually is. Standing seam runs roughly double the asphalt price and lasts roughly twice as long, and it handles low slope sections that shingles do not. On a five year hold, asphalt is the better number.

How long does the work actually take

Most single family roofs are torn off and re covered inside one day. Steep pitch, multiple valleys or standing seam metal run two to four days. The written scope states the expected number of days before you sign.

Do you charge for the inspection or the estimate

No. The inspection and the written estimate are free, and you keep the photographs whether or not you book the work.
