Click-forward animation — three ways the card "comes forward"

Rebuilt after the first three did not read as "coming forward" enough. Each row below plays a much stronger forward motion. Click any card, watch it, then it resets automatically.

A

POP — card leaps out of the row toward you

Click a card

The card scales up to 1.28x and rises off the page with a huge shadow underneath, the other cards go dark and blur back. Feels like the card has been lifted right off the screen toward your face. Springy easing at the end so it "lands" with a subtle bounce.
