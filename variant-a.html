What Jefferson Parish requires before we touch your roof

Metairie is unincorporated Jefferson Parish, so the parish permits your roof, not the City of New Orleans. Four rules decide how the job runs.

Permit

$50, online, no plans

The residential re roofing permit is a flat $50 paid by credit card through the parish MGO Connect portal. No construction plans and no survey are required.

Licence

Over $7,500 needs a licence

From 1 January 2026 any residential roof job valued above $7,500 requires a Louisiana Residential Roofing or Residential Construction licence. That covers almost every full replacement.

Code

2021 IRC applies

Re roofing must comply with the 2021 International Residential Code, which sets the nailing pattern, the underlayment and the edge detail we install to.

Inspection

Photos, not an inspector

The parish does not send an inspector mid job. Every phase is signed off from geo tagged photographs showing the nailing pattern and the material packaging with its wind rating visible.

Metairie roof replacement questions

How much does a new roof cost in Metairie

For a full architectural asphalt replacement, most Metairie homes land between $7,000 and $18,000 depending on size, pitch and how many layers come off. Larger or complex roofs and standing seam metal run $16,000 to $28,000. The Jefferson Parish permit adds a flat $50.

Do I need a permit to replace a roof in Metairie

Yes. Metairie sits in unincorporated Jefferson Parish, which requires a residential re roofing permit. It is $50, paid by card through the parish MGO Connect portal, and it needs no construction plans and no survey. We pull it for you.

Will an inspector come to my house

No. Jefferson Parish verifies re roofing from geo tagged photographs instead of a site visit. Each phase has to be documented with the nailing pattern visible and the material packaging showing its wind rating. We take and file that evidence as part of the job.

Can I get the $10,000 state grant

The Louisiana Fortify Homes Program awards up to $10,000, but it pays for the FORTIFIED upgrade portion of the work rather than the whole roof. It runs as a lottery with limited application windows, so it is worth applying for and never worth budgeting on before you are awarded.

How long does it take

Most Metairie single family roofs are stripped and finished within one working day once materials are on site. Decking replacement or a steep, complex roof can push it into a second day.

Should I repair or replace

If the roof is under about 12 years old and the damage is confined to one slope or one flashing detail, repair. If you are paying for a third repair, or shingles are cupping and shedding granules across the whole roof, replacement is the cheaper number over five years.
