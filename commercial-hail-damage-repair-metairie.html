A commercial roof does not show hail the way a shingle roof does. On a residential roof the evidence is on the surface, which is why residential assessment counts bruises in a marked test square. On a low slope membrane over rigid insulation the stone does something different: it fractures the membrane where the board underneath cannot support it and it crushes the insulation itself, and both of those can sit under a surface that still looks intact from standing height.

That is the single most useful thing to understand before anyone quotes on a Metairie commercial hail job. A walk over the roof establishes almost nothing on its own. The damage is confirmed by opening the assembly at points chosen deliberately across the roof, seeing the membrane, the insulation and the deck in section, and then mapping how far the wet insulation extends from there. Anyone offering a firm price after twenty minutes on the roof has not done that.

Big Easy Roofing does not tell you what your policy does or does not do, and Louisiana law prohibits a contractor from interpreting policy coverage or duties. Those questions belong to your insurer or to a licensed public adjuster. What a roofer can properly do is establish the physical cause, record it thoroughly, and write a good faith itemised and detailed estimate, and that record is what this page is about.

Permit, code and the manufacturer warranty on a hail repair

Do not assume a commercial hail repair in Metairie is permit free. Jefferson Parish publishes a roofing exemption for one and two family dwellings only, and its minor repairs clause needs the Building Permits Department to approve it case by case. We confirm the scope with the parish rather than guessing at it.

Jefferson Parish permit

Confirm the scope

The parish's published no permit list covers a change of roof covering only, and gutter work, on one and two family dwellings. There is no commercial equivalent. A separate clause allows minor ordinary repairs without a permit with the Building Permits Department's approval, which is discretionary rather than automatic.

Stamped plans

Expect them

Jefferson Parish's published commercial submittal requirements call for a complete set of plans by a Louisiana registered architect or civil engineer, with a statement of supervision on the first sheet, and the parish publishes no roofing specific exemption from that. It also notes that circumstances particular to an application may dictate deviations, so the scope is confirmed before anything is filed.

Keeping the manufacturer warranty

Their detail, their material

A membrane still inside its no dollar limit or material warranty can usually be repaired without affecting it, provided the work follows the manufacturer's published detail in their material and any other warranty condition, which often includes using an approved contractor. A patch in the wrong material is the reliable way to cause a problem.

The limit on recovering

Count what is there

The building code does not set a flat cap of two roof coverings. What it does is bar a recover where two or more applications already exist, and a full tear off resets that count. On a hail job that matters, because a roof that has already been recovered once has fewer options than a roof that has not.

Cosmetic against functional on a metal roof

A dented metal panel that has not been breached and whose coating is intact is a different finding from one where the impact has cracked the finish, opened a seam or crushed a fastener seal. The first is an appearance question, the second is a water question, and they are genuinely different things rather than two words for the same damage.

The distinction is contested often enough that it is worth documenting properly either way. We record panel by panel, note where the coating is broken and where it is not, and say plainly which category each area falls into rather than pushing everything into whichever one is more convenient.

What hail leaves behind on a Metairie commercial roof

Three findings recur on Metairie commercial hail surveys, and only one of them is visible from the surface. That is why the survey drives the scope rather than the walk over, and why two roofs with identical looking tops can need completely different work underneath.

Crushed insulation under an intact looking membrane

This is the finding that changes the job. The stone compresses the rigid board under the membrane, the board holds water from that point on, and its thermal performance is materially reduced because crushed insulation does not recover its shape. How much drying potential is left depends on the insulation type, its facer and the membrane above. That membrane can still be shedding water perfectly well.

It only shows up in section, which is why cores are cut and why the moisture survey exists to decide where to cut. A roof that reads sound on a walk over and holds a large wet area on the moisture map is the normal outcome, not an unusual one.

Soft metal and rooftop equipment carrying the clearest evidence

The best physical record of a hail event on a commercial building is often not the roof surface at all. Condenser fins on rooftop HVAC units, vent caps, counterflashing, gutter and downspout, and any painted soft metal will hold impact marks with a clear directional pattern.

We photograph all of it as part of the survey, because it establishes that an impact event reached this specific building and roughly from which direction, which is a physical fact a roofer can properly record.

Damage found at the details rather than the field

Curbs, drains, pitch pockets, skylights and parapet junctions take hail badly because the membrane there is already worked, layered and often unsupported. A field of membrane can survive a storm that opens every detail on the roof.

That is worth knowing before a price is agreed, because detail work is slower per square foot than field repair. A quote built on field area alone will not cover it, and where the leak has already started a Metairie commercial leak detection runs alongside the hail survey.

How do I know if hail damaged my Metairie commercial roof

Often you cannot tell by looking, which is the main difference from a house. On a low slope membrane the stone fractures the sheet over unsupported insulation and crushes the board beneath, and the surface can still look sound. The reliable indicators are impact marks on soft metal and on rooftop equipment, and the confirmation is a core cut that shows the membrane, insulation and deck in section.

How much does commercial hail damage repair cost in Metairie

Estimated ranges across the affected area only, not the whole roof. TPO and PVC run $5 to $12 per square foot, EPDM $4 to $11, modified bitumen and built up $5 to $13, and metal $6 to $14. Each core cut runs an estimated $150 to $400. The depth of insulation being replaced and roof access move the figure more than the membrane type does.

Why do you cut holes in a roof you have been asked to repair

Because it is the only way to see the assembly. A core cut opens membrane, insulation and deck in section at a chosen point, is photographed, and is then patched back to the manufacturer detail. Several are taken across the roof so the result is a map of the damage rather than one reading. The alternative is pricing work on an assumption about what is under the surface.

What is the difference between infrared and a moisture gauge

Infrared reads heat retained by wet material after sunset, so it needs solar loading during the day, a dry exposed surface, low wind and a clear survey window, and ballast, ponding and highly reflective coatings can all make the results unreliable. A nuclear moisture gauge responds to hydrogen and compares counts against a known dry area, works in conditions infrared cannot, and needs a licensed operator because of its radioactive source. Neither reports a percentage of water and neither replaces a core cut.

Do I need a Jefferson Parish permit for a commercial hail repair

Do not assume you do not. The parish publishes a roofing exemption for one and two family dwellings only and there is no commercial equivalent. Its minor ordinary repairs clause allows some work without a permit but only with the Building Permits Department's approval, decided case by case rather than automatically. Big Easy Roofing confirms the scope with the parish before work starts.

Will a hail repair void my roof manufacturer warranty

It should not, provided the repair follows the manufacturer's published detail using their material and meets any other condition in the warranty, which often includes the work being done by an approved contractor. A repair made in the wrong material or by the wrong method is the usual cause of a problem. We quote the compliant method, and where a warranty has already expired we say so plainly.

My metal roof is dented but not leaking, is that damage

It is two different findings and they are worth separating. A dented panel with an intact coating and no breach is an appearance question. A dent that has cracked the finish, opened a seam or crushed a fastener seal is a water question and will shorten the life of the roof. We survey panel by panel and record which category each area falls into rather than pushing everything into one.

When does hail damage stop being a repair

When the wet crushed insulation has spread past the point where cutting it out makes sense. Isolated wet areas are cut out and replaced. Widespread wet insulation usually has poor drying potential and materially reduced thermal performance, so money spent on repairs is going into a deck that needs recovering or replacing. The moisture map and the core cuts answer this, not the age of the roof.
