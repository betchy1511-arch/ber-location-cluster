Slidell roof insurance claim scope at a glance

An insurance-involved Slidell roof scope sits inside a Louisiana statute that bars a roofing contractor from interpreting the policy. Big Easy Roofing documents the roof, writes a scope the insurer can read, and photographs the work; the policy reading is between the owner, the insurer and a public adjuster if one is engaged.

What we do

Document, scope, install

We inspect and photograph the roof condition, write a scope that references the city's adopted codes, obtain the city permit and photograph each weatherproofing detail during install. The documentation is designed for the insurer to read.

What we do not do

Interpret your policy

La. R.S. 37:2159.1 bars a Louisiana residential roofing contractor from interpreting insurance coverage on a roof claim. We do not quote ACV or RCV, decide deductibles or negotiate the claim; the policy reading is between the owner, the insurer and (if separately engaged) a licensed public adjuster.

Public adjuster is a separate role

R.S. 22:1706(H)(11)

A Louisiana-licensed public adjuster represents the policyholder on the claim. The statute keeps that role separate from the contractor on the same loss. Big Easy Roofing does not act as both; where a public adjuster is engaged, that is a separate contract with a separate licence.

Insurers read condition evidence, scopes that reference codes, and install photographs. The scope on this page is written for that audience without crossing into interpretation of what the policy will pay.

The owner remains the decision-maker

On their own policy

The owner picks the carrier, reads the policy, works with the adjuster and signs the settlement. The written scope supports that decision with evidence; it does not make the decision.
