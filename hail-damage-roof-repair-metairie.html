Why an independent inspection matters

An insurer's field adjuster is trained to spot hail impact but is not the roofer who will do the work. An independent hail impact inspection from Big Easy Roofing runs on the same 10 by 10 foot test square method the adjuster uses, and the two counts are usually within one grade.

When hail damage becomes a full replacement

Impact damage on a Metairie shingle roof usually becomes a full replacement rather than a repair once two conditions are met. First, the count exceeds eight impacts per test square on any slope. Second, the shingle line is no longer available for a matching patch, which is common on roofs more than 8 years old.

Any decision on that scale is priced against a full replacement and written into an amended scope before the crew continues on your Metairie roof. Nothing is decided on the ladder.

The hail damage tells on a Metairie shingle roof

Hail damage on a Metairie roof rarely looks the way homeowners expect. A hail bruise is a round dark spot on the shingle mat where the granules have been knocked off, not a visible dent from the ground. Three tells cover almost every real claim in this postcode, and each is easy to miss without a ladder and a chalked test square.

Granule loss in the downspout catch

The first honest sign of a hail event is a pile of shingle granules at the base of every downspout. Metairie's rainfall washes them off a damaged roof and deposits them in a visible line. If the granule pile is significant across multiple downspouts, the roof deserves an inspection.

This is the cheapest early warning available and does not require going near the roof. A phone photograph of the granule pile is often the first item in the claim pack.

Round dark bruises on shingle tabs

A hail bruise reads as a round spot roughly the size of a nickel where the granules have been knocked off the mat, exposing the asphalt beneath. Bruises spread randomly across a slope rather than in the linear pattern of foot traffic or a fallen limb.

Louisiana insurers count these on a 10 by 10 foot chalked test square. Eight or more per square on any slope typically triggers a full replacement decision.

Dented soft metal on drip edge and flashings

Aluminium drip edge along the eave, gutter and downspout components, and metal roof vents show hail impact as clean rounded dents that are visible from the ground. If the soft metal is dented, the shingles above almost always have matching impact damage even when the shingles look intact from the ground.

Insurers accept dented soft metal as corroborating evidence, which is why the claim pack always includes a photograph of the drip edge and every roof vent.

In nearly every Louisiana homeowners policy, hail is a named covered peril. The claim is settled based on impact count per test square, the age of the roof, and the terms of the policy. What does an insurer count as hail damage

Most Louisiana insurers use a 10 by 10 foot chalked test square on each slope. Eight or more clean impact marks in that square typically triggers a full slope or full roof replacement decision. Fewer impacts may still qualify for a repair. Cosmetic granule loss without mat damage is rarely covered.

Does the Louisiana Fortify Homes Program cover hail damage repair

The Fortify Homes Program grant of up to $10,000 covers a full FORTIFIED standard roof replacement, not a repair. Where hail damage triggers a full replacement, the grant may cover the FORTIFIED upgrade cost on top of the insurance payout. Yes, and it is one of the smartest things a Metairie homeowner can do after a claim. What is a hail damage supplement claim

A supplement claim is a follow up to the original claim, submitted when the crew finds hidden damage during the repair that the adjuster did not see on the initial inspection. Common examples are deck damage under lifted shingles or damaged underlayment. Big Easy Roofing documents the finding and submits the supplement to your insurer on your behalf.
