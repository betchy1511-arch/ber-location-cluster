Baton Rouge commercial roof replacement
at a glance
A commercial replacement is priced on area, assembly and what is found when the membrane comes up, so it is surveyed before it is quoted rather than priced from a plan. The permit is the one figure the parish publishes, and it is calculated from the value of the contract rather than charged as a flat roofing fee.

Which code applies
IBC Chapter 15
Act 239 of 2025 added La. R.S. 40:1730.23(K). Subsection (K)(1) routes every building that is not a one or two family dwelling to International Building Code Chapter 15, the roof assemblies chapter, as adopted with Louisiana amendments.

What decides the price
The insulation, not the membrane
Membrane cost per square foot varies far less between systems than the cost of what is underneath it. Saturated insulation found at tear off is the largest single variable on a commercial replacement, which is why we measure it before quoting rather than after.
Warranty
Two of them, and they differ
Our workmanship warranty covers the installation. The membrane manufacturer issues its own warranty on the system, on its own terms, after its own inspection. They are separate documents and one does not stand in for the other.
A commercial roof is an assembly rather than a covering. Deck, vapour control, insulation, cover board, membrane and every flashing and penetration through it are one system, and a replacement is the only moment when the parts below the membrane can be corrected at all. That is why a replacement quote that names only a membrane is describing a fraction of the job it is pricing.
The decision that sets everything else is whether the existing roof comes off. A recover lays a new membrane over what is there, which is quicker and cheaper and leaves any wet insulation in place under the new roof. A tear off takes the assembly back to the deck, which costs more, takes longer and is the only version that lets anyone see the deck. Neither is automatically right. What settles it is the condition of the insulation, the number of coverings already on the building, and what the deck is doing at the perimeter.

Since August 2025 a commercial reroof here
leaves two separate records
This part has changed and may not yet be familiar to every building owner. A Baton Rouge commercial replacement now generates a parish permit file and a manufacturer warranty file covering the same roof, at the same stages, to different requirements. Plan for both at the start, and ask for any documentation cost to be named on the quote.
The permit route
R.S. 40:1730.23(K)(1)
Act 239 of 2025 requires any parish or municipality that issues construction permits to permit and inspect roof construction and reroofing. For a building that is not a one or two family dwelling the code named is International Building Code Chapter 15.
The parish verifies from photographs
Filed through MyGovernmentOnline
La. R.S. 40:1730.23(J) allows a code enforcement officer to accept location verified geotagged photographs or video for roofing inspections. East Baton Rouge is what turns that permission into a checklist, and its Roofing Information Form and photograph set are filed as one PDF through MyGovernmentOnline.
The warranty is a separate record
Issued by the manufacturer
A system warranty on a single ply roof is issued by the membrane manufacturer, usually after its own inspection and only where the installer holds its approval. It is not the same document as the permit file and neither one satisfies the other.
Which is an argument for settling both first
Before the tear off starts
Both records are made from work that is only visible while the roof is open. Deciding at the survey who photographs what, and to whose requirements, costs nothing. Reconstructing it after the membrane is welded down is not possible.
The parish checklist is written in residential terms
The East Baton Rouge Roofing Information Form asks for the deck, underlayment and roofing material to be photographed in accordance with the 2021 IRC and state amendments. The IRC is the residential code, and Act 239 (K)(1) routes a commercial building to IBC Chapter 15 instead. The form also asks for a starter strip photograph, which a single ply membrane does not have, and asks whether any portion of the roof is below 4:12 pitch, which on a low slope commercial roof is generally all of it.
That is not a reason to expect a problem. It is a reason to agree the documentation path with Permits and Inspections before the work starts rather than at the inspection, which is how we approach a commercial job here.
What a recover leaves behind
A recover is a legitimate choice and it is often the right one. What it cannot do is remove water. Insulation that is already saturated stays saturated under the new membrane, keeps its weight, keeps its reduced thermal value, and keeps working on the deck underneath where nobody can see it.
It also matters how many coverings the building already carries, because the reroofing provisions of the code limit how many can be laid over one another. Where the count is unclear from the roof itself, a core cut settles it, and that is a small job compared with finding out at the wrong moment.

Replacing a roof over
a building that is still open
A commercial replacement usually has to be carried out over people who are still working, trading or worshipping underneath it. That constraint shapes the programme more than the membrane choice does, and it is worth being explicit about it before a start date is agreed rather than after.
Dry in every day, not every phase
The rule that governs an occupied replacement is that whatever is opened is closed and watertight before the crew leaves. That sets the size of each day area, and it is why an occupied roof takes longer than the same area on an empty building.
It also means the programme has to carry weather contingency honestly. A phase plan that assumes clear days between June and September in south Louisiana is a plan that will move, so it is better to build the float in at the start than to explain it later.
Rooftop plant is part of the roof
Condensers, ducts, kitchen exhaust and their curbs and supports all pass through the membrane, and each penetration is a detail that has to be reflashed rather than worked around. Units that need lifting or temporary isolation belong in the programme with the mechanical contractor named, not discovered on the day.
Grease from a kitchen exhaust is the specific case worth raising early, because it rules certain membranes out rather than merely shortening their life, and it is a common reason a like for like replacement is the wrong specification.
Drainage is the cheapest thing to fix and the easiest to skip
A replacement is the one opportunity to correct falls, add outlets and deal with ponding, and it is the part most quotes leave silent because it adds cost to a competitive number. Standing water is not automatically a defect, but water still on a roof long after the rain has stopped is telling you something about the falls or the outlets.
Tapered insulation, additional outlets or overflow provision are separate lines on a proper scope. Ask for them to be priced even if the decision is to leave them, because a quote that never mentions drainage has priced the membrane and not the roof.

How much does a commercial roof replacement cost in Baton Rouge
We do not publish a square foot range for commercial replacement, because the area, the assembly and the condition of the insulation move the figure too far for a range to be useful. What we will do is survey the roof, take core cuts where the build up is unclear, and price the membrane, the insulation, the drainage and the flashings as separate lines so the quote can be compared with anyone else.
Why is the permit cost different on every commercial job
Because East Baton Rouge has no flat roofing fee. A commercial permit is calculated from the construction valuation, with a separate plan review fee alongside it, so the permit follows the contract price. The current bands are on the parish fee page, and a technology fee applies to every permit.
Should I tear off or recover
It depends on three things, and none of them can be judged from the ground: whether the insulation is dry, how many coverings the building already carries, and what the deck is doing at the perimeter. A recover is quicker and cheaper and it cannot remove water, so where the insulation is saturated it locks the problem in under a new membrane. Core cuts settle it.
Which membrane should I choose
TPO and PVC suit large open roofs where a reflective surface is wanted, PVC specifically where grease or chemical exposure is present, EPDM where simplicity and a long track record matter, and modified bitumen on smaller or heavily detailed roofs with foot traffic. Detailing and drainage decide how long any of them last far more than the choice between them does.
Does a commercial roof replacement need a permit in East Baton Rouge
Yes, for work beginning on or after 1 August 2025. Act 239 of 2025 added La. R.S. 40:1730.23(K), which requires parishes and municipalities that issue construction permits to permit and inspect roof construction and reroofing, and East Baton Rouge applies it to commercial and residential structures alike.
My building is inside St. George, does that change anything
It changes who issues the permit. Permitting inside the City of St. George limits transferred to St. George for applications filed on or after 16 October 2024, so an address there is not permitted by the parish. Baker, Zachary and Central are separate municipalities within the parish as well. The address decides the route, so it is the first thing we check.
Which code is a commercial roof inspected against
International Building Code Chapter 15, the roof assemblies and rooftop structures chapter. Act 239 splits it explicitly: subsection (K)(1) sends buildings other than one and two family dwellings to the IBC, and only (K)(2) sends houses to the residential code. Louisiana is on the 2021 editions with state amendments, with the 2024 editions proposed for 1 January 2027.
What is the difference between your warranty and the manufacturer warranty
Ours covers our workmanship on the installation. The manufacturer warranty covers the system and is issued by the membrane manufacturer on its own terms, usually after its own inspection and only where the installer holds its approval. They are two separate documents and neither stands in for the other, which is worth knowing before a quote is compared on warranty length alone.
Can other trades work on the new roof afterwards
They can, and it is a common way a commercial roof gets damaged after it is finished. A mechanical or telecoms contractor cutting in a new penetration without the roofer involved creates a detail nobody has flashed and nobody has recorded. Agree a route for rooftop work at handover and keep it in the building file.
How long will the building be disrupted
The programme is set by how much roof can be opened and made watertight in a day, because on an occupied building nothing is left open overnight. That is what makes an occupied replacement longer than the same area on an empty one, and it is why we set the day areas and the weather contingency before agreeing a start date rather than after.
